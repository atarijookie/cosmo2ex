Cosmo2ex

Android floppy app

Switch floppy images from your phone, in the app or with a home screen widget.

1. Install the app

Download the app

Open this page on the phone. Android asks you to allow installing from your browser, and warns that the app is not verified - that's expected, it doesn't come from Google Play.

The app is not included in this build (it's created by android/build.sh).
