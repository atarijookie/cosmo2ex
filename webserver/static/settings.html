Floppy images download

One more thing before you start:

Cosmo2ex can download floppy images (games, demos) from a list on the internet to ... - a while after it starts and then once a day. Images downloaded before are not downloaded again, and at least 1 GB is always kept free on the disk. Without an image in the drive, the device and the floppy page start browsing the images there.
