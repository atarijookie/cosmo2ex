Logging

Applies to ce_cores and webserver immediately. It's not stored - after restart LOG_LEVEL from .env is used again.
Wipe empties all current logs and deletes the rotated (.1) ones - e.g. before reproducing a problem.

SLM laser printer

Paper in the cassette of the emulated SLM 605/804 printer (an ACSI ID set to SLM 605/804), for all devices. Applies from the next printer command. The SLM804 emulator DIAB630.PRG works only with Letter, GDOS drivers, Signum and Papyrus use the selected size.

brightness
Color of the LED once the device is connected and running (on the floppy: with an image loaded), for all devices. Sent to the connected devices right away, others get it when they connect. The LED shows the colors only roughly. Other colors stay as they are: orange while starting, red blinking on error, and on the floppy blue in WiFi setup and light blue while the button is held.
