Welcome!

Please double check that you have installed the IKBD Injector in your ST as well as the keyboard injection cable from Cosmo2ex to the ST.

Now you can remote control your ST with this webapp.

  • move your finger on this webpage to move your STs mouse
  • double tap with one finger to left click on the ST
  • double tap with two fingers to doubleclick on the ST
  • right mouse button is not supported yet on touch devices
  • use the ST Keyboard image to type. Just tap the Key you want to enter. You can tap multiple keys (e.g. CTRL ALT DEL for reset if your TOS supports it)
  • hide ST keyboard with "Hide ST keyboard"
  • show the touch devices keyboard with "Toggle system keyboard"
  • "Show joysticks" shows two joysticks at the bottom right - tap the arrows and FIRE to send them to the ST
  • recieve a screencast from ST (you must have CE_DD.PRG running on the ST) by tapping "Start Screencast", or a single screenshot by tapping "Take Screenshot"

Gamepads connected to this device work as ST joysticks: press a button on the gamepad to activate it. The first one activated is the first joystick, the second one the second joystick. Stick or D-pad moves, any other button is fire.

ST Screencast

This is a highly experimental feature and will cause instabilities with older software. You will need a bleeding edge browser for this (read: Chrome or Safari). It only works with "clean" ST software, it will not work with games or demos.

Problems? Read this. If this doesn't solve the issue, send a mail!

Welcome!

Please double check that you have installed the IKBD Injector in your ST as well as the keyboard injection cable from Cosmo2ex to the ST.

Now you can remote control your ST with this webapp.

  • move your mouse on this webpage to move your STs mouse
  • left click in the browser to left click on the ST
  • right click in the browser to right click on the ST
  • click "Grab mouse" (if available in your browser) to attach your mouse to the browser, so you won't leave its boundaries. Press ESC to leave this mode. While grabbed, the mouse wheel sends cursor up / down to the ST.
  • use your PCs keyboard to type
  • when "Enable Keys 0" / "Enable Keys 1" is on in the IKBD settings, those keys move joystick 0 / 1 instead of typing - the line under the settings shows which keys they are
  • for Special Keys you can click "Show ST keyboard" to show a ST Keyboard. Just click the Key you want to enter
  • "Show joysticks" shows two joysticks at the bottom right - click the arrows and FIRE to send them to the ST. They also light up what your gamepads and joystick keys are doing
  • the bar at the bottom sends special characters like @ { } \ as they are - select your ST's (TOS) keyboard layout there. "Keys: by position" sends your keys like a real ST keyboard would (the ST's layout applies), "Keys: by character" sends the character your PC typed, translated for the ST layout
  • recieve a screencast from ST (you must have CE_DD.PRG running on the ST) by clicking "Start Screencast", or a single screenshot by clicking "Take Screenshot"

Gamepads connected to this computer work as ST joysticks: press a button on the gamepad to activate it. The first one activated is the first joystick, the second one the second joystick. Stick or D-pad moves, any other button is fire. They only work while this window has the focus.

ST Screencast

This is a highly experimental feature and will cause instabilities with older software. You will need a bleeding edge browser for this (read: Chromium or Chrome). It only works with "clean" ST software, it will not work with games or demos. It will only work with the most current HDDRIVER.

Problems? Read this. If this doesn't solve the issue, send a mail!

Please use a better browser!
